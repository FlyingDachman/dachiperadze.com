Football law & advisory

Your career in football.
A sound legal footing.

I advise players considering a contract or seeking payment, clubs negotiating transfers or addressing disputes, and agents reviewing representation agreements and the obligations they create.

01

Playing contracts & transfers

I review and negotiate employment, renewal and transfer terms, including remuneration, performance incentives, release clauses and termination rights. The assessment considers how these provisions operate together and where responsibility lies between the parties.

02

Agent & commercial agreements

I examine representation, sponsorship and image-rights agreements beyond their headline terms. Authority, exclusivity, duration and continuing obligations deserve particular scrutiny where they could restrict a subsequent move or commercial opportunity.

03

Disputes & regulatory questions

For disputes over payment or contractual performance, and for disciplinary matters, I examine the evidence, applicable procedure and appropriate forum. Where FIFA proceedings or sports arbitration are relevant, I assess jurisdiction, admissibility and the deadlines that must be observed.

I welcome enquiries worldwide. Where a matter involves several countries, I consider the governing law, jurisdiction and any need for local counsel before agreeing the scope of work.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • Your role and the clubs or agents involved
  • The countries connected with the contract or transfer
  • Whether the matter concerns a proposal, an existing agreement or a dispute
  • Any signing date, transfer deadline or procedural deadline

A concise account of the matter is sufficient for an initial discussion. We can then agree how to share any confidential documents and, if I can assist, establish the scope and terms of the engagement.
