Football law & advisory

The agreements.
The implications.

For players, the priority may be a proposed contract or an unresolved obligation. For clubs, it may be a transfer, contractual documentation or a dispute. For agents, it may be the terms of an instruction or the enforceability of a representation arrangement.

01

Playing contracts & transfers

I review and negotiate the contractual terms surrounding employment, renewals and transfers. The assessment considers remuneration provisions, performance incentives, release and termination clauses, and the allocation of responsibilities between the parties.

02

Agent & commercial agreements

Representation, sponsorship and image-rights arrangements warrant scrutiny beyond the headline terms. I examine the scope of authority, exclusivity, contractual duration and commercial obligations, with particular attention to provisions that may constrain a subsequent move.

03

Disputes & regulatory questions

Where payments, contractual performance or disciplinary proceedings are in dispute, I assess the documents, procedural position and appropriate forum. Matters involving FIFA or sports arbitration require an assessment of jurisdiction, admissibility and the applicable deadlines.
