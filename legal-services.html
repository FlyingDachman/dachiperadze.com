FAQ

Before you instruct.

Can I pay for Contract Review online?

Contract Review is a defined service priced at €300. Payment is made through Stripe. The matter remains subject to scope and conflict checks, and secure document-sharing instructions are provided separately. For direct-purchase legal services, the stated fee covers the defined scope shown on the service card; any additional or materially expanded work is agreed separately.

Why are FIFA and CAS matters priced “from” a figure?

The work can vary substantially depending on jurisdiction, deadlines, evidence, procedural history, number of parties and the amount of written work required. The starting fee gives an indication without pretending that every case is the same. FIFA Football Tribunal and CAS representation therefore begin with an assessment.

Does a consultation create a lawyer–client relationship?

No. A consultation is an opportunity to discuss the matter and determine the appropriate next step. A formal professional relationship begins when the matter is accepted and the engagement is confirmed.

Can you review a football agency representation agreement?

Yes. Agency representation agreements can be reviewed, drafted or revised as a separate legal mandate. Where a matter involves an existing or proposed agency relationship, conflicts are checked before any advice is given.
