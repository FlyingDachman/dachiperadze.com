Tennis law & advisory

On court and beyond.
Your commercial interests.

I advise players on endorsements, representation and disputes, and help agents, clubs and organisations assess their contractual relationships with players, commercial partners and professional service providers.

01

Sponsorship & image rights

I review endorsement and sponsorship terms, including exclusivity, use of a player’s name and likeness, appearance commitments and termination rights. I explain the practical effect of those obligations before the agreement is signed.

02

Representation & support arrangements

I assess agreements with agents, management teams and other professional service providers, examining the authority granted, responsibilities assumed and duration of the relationship. Particular attention is paid to overlapping commitments and the consequences of termination.

03

Contractual & regulatory matters

When a commercial dispute or regulatory issue arises, I examine the agreement, correspondence and applicable procedure. I explain the legal position and identify the decisions and deadlines that require your attention.

Based in Tbilisi, Georgia, I welcome enquiries from across Europe, Asia and North Africa. Where a matter involves several countries, I consider the governing law, jurisdiction and any need for local counsel before agreeing the scope of work.

Useful details for your enquiry

  • Your role and the player or organisation concerned
  • The proposed or existing commercial relationship
  • The countries and counterparties involved
  • Any signing date, appearance commitment or procedural deadline

A concise account of the matter is sufficient for an initial discussion. We can then agree how to share any confidential documents and, if I can assist, establish the scope and terms of the engagement.
