Tennis law & advisory

The agreements.
The implications.

Players can enquire about a proposed endorsement, representation agreement or dispute. Agents, clubs and organisations can seek advice on their contractual relationships with players, commercial counterparties and professional service providers.

01

Sponsorship & image rights

I review endorsement and sponsorship terms, including exclusivity, permitted uses of a player’s name and likeness, appearance commitments and termination provisions. The aim is to make the commercial obligations intelligible before the agreement is concluded.

02

Representation & support arrangements

Agreements with agents, management teams and other professional service providers should define authority, responsibilities and duration with precision. I examine the boundaries of each instruction, overlapping commitments and the consequences of ending the relationship.

03

Contractual & regulatory matters

Where a commercial relationship becomes contentious or a regulatory question arises, I assess the relevant agreement, correspondence and procedural position. Advice is framed around the facts, the applicable framework and the decisions that require attention.
