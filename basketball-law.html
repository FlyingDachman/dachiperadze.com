Basketball law & advisory

The agreements.
The implications.

Players can seek advice before signing or when an existing relationship deteriorates. Clubs can enquire about contractual documentation, negotiations and disputes. Agents can discuss representation terms and issues arising from an international placement.

01

Player contracts & international moves

I review proposed terms and negotiate the allocation of contractual risk. Salary provisions, bonuses, release arrangements, injury-related clauses and termination rights are considered in the context of the agreement as a whole and the move being contemplated.

02

Representation & sponsorship

An agent or sponsorship agreement can extend beyond a single season. I examine authority, exclusivity, duration and continuing obligations so that the parties understand how the arrangement may affect future negotiations and commercial opportunities.

03

Payment & contractual disputes

An unpaid obligation or an early termination calls for a careful examination of the contract and evidence. I assess the dispute-resolution clause, relevant correspondence and procedural deadlines before advising on the available course of action.
