01 / The practice

Independent advice.
Personal attention.

I am Dachi Peradze, a sports and football lawyer based in Tbilisi, Georgia. My practice serves athletes, clubs, agents and sports organisations seeking advice on contracts, disputes, commercial arrangements and sporting regulations.

My football law practice covers playing contracts, transfers, agent agreements, unpaid salaries and disputes before FIFA and CAS, subject to the relevant jurisdictional requirements.

Whether you are considering a new agreement or facing a dispute, I examine the facts, the applicable rules and the consequences of each available course of action.

I assess each instruction personally and remain your point of contact as the matter develops. My advice is candid, my recommendations are reasoned, and the scope of the work is agreed with you from the outset.

Before instructing

Before we begin.

Where is your sports law practice based?

I am based in Tbilisi, Georgia, and welcome enquiries about both Georgian and international sporting matters. Athletes, clubs, agents and sports organisations can contact me directly by WhatsApp, text or email.

Which sports does your practice cover?

My practice covers football, basketball, tennis, rugby and esports. I assess each instruction against the relevant sporting rules, jurisdiction and circumstances.

Do you accept international instructions?

Yes. Before accepting an international instruction, I consider the governing law, jurisdiction and appropriate forum, together with any need for local counsel or another specialist.

What should my initial enquiry include?

Please include your name, sport, a concise description of the issue and any relevant deadline. We can agree how to share documents after the initial discussion.

Will you handle my matter personally?

Yes. I assess your matter personally and remain your point of contact throughout the engagement. If assistance from local counsel or another specialist is needed, I discuss this with you.
