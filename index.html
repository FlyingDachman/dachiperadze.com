Before instructing

Before we begin.

Which sports does your practice cover?

I advise on matters arising in football, basketball, tennis, rugby and esports. Each instruction is assessed in light of the relevant sporting rules, jurisdiction and circumstances.

Do you accept international instructions?

I am based in Tbilisi, Georgia, and welcome international enquiries. Before accepting an instruction, I consider jurisdiction, the appropriate forum and whether local counsel or another specialist is required.

What should my initial enquiry include?

Please provide your name, sport, a concise account of the issue and any relevant deadline. Arrangements for sharing documents can be agreed following our initial discussion.

Will you handle my matter personally?

Yes. I remain your direct point of contact throughout the engagement. Work begins once I have accepted the instruction and we have agreed the terms.
