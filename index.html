A few practical details

Before we begin.

Which sports do you work with?

My practice focuses on football, basketball, tennis, rugby and esports, supporting athletes, clubs, agents and sports organisations. The relevant rules and jurisdiction are assessed for each matter.

Can I contact you about an international matter?

Yes. I’m based in Tbilisi, Georgia, and welcome international enquiries. I first review the applicable forum and jurisdiction, and determine whether local counsel or another specialist is needed.

What should I include in my first message?

Your name, sport, a short description of the issue and any upcoming deadline are a good starting point. Document-sharing arrangements can be confirmed after the initial conversation.

Will I work with you directly?

Yes. You contact me directly, and I remain your point of contact for the agreed work. A professional engagement begins once the matter is accepted and the terms are confirmed.
